PawConnect

PawConnect helps you discover pets from local shelters that suit your home and lifestyle, and apply to adopt them online. Shelters can manage their pets and adoption applications in one place.

How adoption works

Adopting through PawConnect takes four key steps.

  1. Available now

    1. Browse pets

    Look through the pets currently available for adoption and open a profile to learn more.

  2. Available now

    2. Submit an application

    Select "Apply to Adopt" on a pet's profile and enter your details and reason for adopting.

  3. Planned for a later release

    3. Shelter review

    Shelter staff review your application and update its status.

  4. Planned for a later release

    4. Track application progress

    Use your application reference to check the current status of your application.
